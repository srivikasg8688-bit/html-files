About Free Fire

Free Fire is a battle royale game where players fight against other players and try to become the last survivor.

Players can collect weapons, equipment and supplies while moving through the battlefield. Strategy, movement and accuracy are important for survival.

Most Used SMG Guns

  4. MAC10
